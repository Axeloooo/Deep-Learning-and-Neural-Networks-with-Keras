# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This project welcomes learners and contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Everyone should be able to ask questions, share work, and receive constructive feedback without harassment or discrimination, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, nationality, appearance, race, religion, sexual orientation, or experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and considerate, especially when someone is learning.
- Give specific, constructive feedback about the work and explain your reasoning.
- Accept corrections in good faith, acknowledge mistakes, and credit others' contributions.
- Respect privacy and personal boundaries.
- Keep discussions relevant and make space for different perspectives.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, personal attacks, sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention, and deliberate intimidation are not acceptable. Neither are publishing private information without permission, persistent disruption, spam, or retaliation against someone who reports a concern or helps resolve it.

Technical disagreement is welcome when expressed respectfully. Repeatedly targeting or belittling a person is not constructive disagreement.

## Scope

This code applies to the repository's issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces. It also applies when someone represents the project publicly. Maintainers and contributors are subject to the same expectations.

## Reporting concerns

Contact the repository owner, [Axeloooo](https://github.com/Axeloooo), to report a concern. Use a private contact method listed on the owner's GitHub profile when available. This repository does not currently publish a dedicated reporting email address.

If no private contact method is available, use [GitHub's abuse-reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) for abusive content or behavior on GitHub. You can also use that route if your concern involves the repository owner. Reports to GitHub are handled by GitHub and do not necessarily notify the repository owner.

For a non-sensitive moderation request, you may open a repository issue asking the owner to review a public interaction. Do not put private evidence or personal information in a public issue. When reporting privately, include relevant links, a brief description of what happened, and any context needed to understand the concern.

## Enforcement

The repository owner is responsible for reviewing project conduct concerns and responding according to their severity and context. Responses may include requesting a correction, removing inappropriate content, issuing a warning, restricting participation temporarily, or permanently blocking participation for serious or repeated violations.

Reports should be handled discreetly, with information shared only as needed to assess and address the concern. Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed. Retaliation for making a good-faith report is itself a violation of this code.

Anyone affected by a moderation decision may ask the owner to reconsider through the same contact route, providing relevant context or new information. GitHub's own moderation decisions are subject to GitHub's processes.
